01 — About

Engineer across the whole stack

I'm Bibhujit Panigrahi, a developer who enjoys the full arc of a product: modelling the data, training the model, wiring the API, and then obsessing over the interface people actually touch.

My repositories split neatly into two halves. On the web side I've built AspireLearn — a React + Spring Boot e-learning platform with OTP email auth, enrollments and an embedded video player — and WanderLust, a full MVC Express + MongoDB rental marketplace with Passport auth, Joi validation and Cloudinary uploads.

On the machine-learning side I've trained and compared classifiers for heart-stroke risk (96.96% accuracy with Logistic Regression across 5 algorithms), built a five-model diabetes prediction service behind a Flask UI, and shipped a retail shrinkage predictor that bridges a Python model into a Node.js/EJS dashboard.

I also like going low-level — a C++ Linux system monitor reading straight from /proc keeps me honest about what's under the abstractions.
